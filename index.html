About Our Musallah

A Dedicated Space for Prayer & Unity

Main Square Musallah was established to serve the spiritual needs of residents, workers, and visitors in the Main Square and Danforth corridor. We offer a clean, peaceful environment for Muslims to perform their daily obligations.

To ensure harmony with our neighbors in the residential complex, access is strictly limited to prayer times.

  • ✓ Open for all 5 daily congregational prayers
  • ✓ Single Friday Jummah congregation at 2:00 PM
  • ✓ Conveniently accessible via TTC and GO Transit
  • ✓ Elevator access to the prayer floor
